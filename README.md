# AI Prompts: The Basics

A free two-session beginner workshop on writing clear prompts for AI assistants. Built entirely from free resources published by Microsoft, Google, OpenAI, Anthropic, and Learn Prompting.

**Instructor:** Dr. Carelicha Duckworth, PhD

| Session | Date | Length | Topics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Session 1 | Tuesday, November 10, 2026 | 3 hours | What AI can and cannot do, the four-part prompt, prompts for writing |
| Session 2 | Thursday, November 12, 2026 | 3 hours | Prompts for planning, prompts with data and documents, using AI responsibly, Microsoft Learn trophy |

Course site: https://drduckworthtri-c.github.io/ai-prompts-basics/

## For students

- [Student Workbook](https://github.com/DrDuckworthTri-C/ai-prompts-basics/raw/main/student/Student_Workbook.docx) (Word): six lessons with steps, copy-ready prompts, labs, and self-checks
- [Prompt Library Template](https://github.com/DrDuckworthTri-C/ai-prompts-basics/raw/main/student/Prompt_Library_Template.docx) (Word): ten entries to fill in during the workshop
- [Sample Data](https://github.com/DrDuckworthTri-C/ai-prompts-basics/raw/main/student/Sample_Data_Expenses.xlsx) (Excel): expense rows for Lesson 5
- [Course Calendar](https://github.com/DrDuckworthTri-C/ai-prompts-basics/raw/main/student/Course_Calendar.docx) (Word): November calendar, session agendas, key dates

### Before the first session

1. Sign in to [Microsoft Copilot](https://copilot.microsoft.com/) with a free Microsoft account. Any free AI assistant works for the exercises.
2. Sign in to [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/) with the same account.
3. Download the workbook and the prompt library template.
